About Rato Studio

Small studio.
Full attention.

Rato Studio is a one-person web design and development studio in Johannesburg. When you work with us, you deal directly with the person who designs and builds your website.

The founder

Meet Olorato.

Rato Studio was founded by Olorato Motlhabane, a Johannesburg freelancer with a passion for technology, problem-solving and ethical hacking.

It started early. At 12, Olorato was exploring hacking, running other people's tools to see what they could do. By the teenage years that had turned into opening those tools up and editing them, to understand how they really worked.

That curiosity became a craft. Olorato started building websites in 2024, taking on more demanding work with every project, and Rato Studio grew out of it.

Today Olorato builds websites for startups and growing businesses, while training towards a career in penetration testing and software engineering. That background shapes how we build: with attention to how a site works under the surface, and not only to how it looks.

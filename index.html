FAQ

Questions? Answers.

How much does a website cost?

Once-off packages are R2 999 (Launch) and R4 999 (Launch Pro). Monthly packages start from R500 a month (Starter Kickstart) and from R600 a month (Business Growth). Bigger or more unusual projects get a custom quote after a free call.

What is the difference between paying once and paying monthly?

With a once-off package you pay for the design and build, and the website is yours once the final payment is made. With a monthly package you pay a smaller amount each month instead of a larger amount upfront, and your site stays online for as long as your subscription is active.

How long does a website take?

Most websites are ready in 1 to 2 weeks, counted from when we have your first payment and your content. Bigger or custom projects can take longer, and we agree the timeline with you before we start.

How does payment work?

For once-off packages you pay a 50% deposit to start and the remaining 50% before your site goes live. Monthly packages are billed every month. We send you an invoice with the payment details.

Which platform should I choose: Framer, WordPress or GoDaddy?

All three give you a professional website. Framer suits modern, design-led sites with smooth animation. WordPress is the most flexible and suits sites that will keep growing. GoDaddy is the simplest to look after yourself. Not sure? Pick any one and we will recommend the best fit on your call.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your logo, the wording for your pages, your photos, and any brand colours or examples of websites you like. If something is missing, tell us on your call and we will plan around it.

Are my domain name and hosting included?

The once-off packages cover the design and build of your site. Your domain, hosting and any platform subscription (Framer, WordPress hosting or GoDaddy) are paid directly to that provider. For monthly packages, we confirm exactly what is included on your call, before any work starts.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Launch includes 1 round of changes and 14 days of support after launch. Launch Pro includes 2 rounds of changes and 30 days of support. Anything beyond that is quoted before we do it, so there are no surprises.

Can I cancel a monthly package?

Yes. There is no minimum term. Give us 30 days' notice by email and your subscription ends. A monthly website is provided as part of the subscription, so it goes offline when the subscription ends.

Do you give refunds?

Your deposit pays for the first stage of work, so it is not refundable once work has started. If you cancel partway through, you pay only for the work done up to that point. The full details are in our terms and conditions.

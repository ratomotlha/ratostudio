1. About these terms

These terms apply to all work done by Rato Studio ("we", "us", "our") for a client ("you"). Rato Studio is a web design and development studio based in South Africa.

By paying a deposit, starting a monthly package or asking us in writing to begin work, you agree to these terms. If we give you a written quote or proposal and it says something different, the quote or proposal applies for that point.

2. Our services

We design and build websites. What is included depends on the package you choose (Launch, Launch Pro, Starter Kickstart or Business Growth) or on your written custom quote.

Anything not listed in your package or quote is extra work. We will always give you a price for extra work and wait for your go-ahead before we do it.

3. Prices

All prices are in South African rand (ZAR).

  • Once-off packages have a fixed price for the work listed in the package.
  • Monthly prices shown on our website are starting prices. Your exact monthly price, and what it includes, is confirmed in writing before any work starts.
  • Custom projects are priced in a written quote.

4. Payment

Once-off packages. A 50% deposit is due before work starts. The remaining 50% is due before your website goes live. A website is not published or handed over until it has been paid for in full.

Monthly packages. You are billed every month, in advance, for as long as your subscription runs. If a payment is more than 7 days late, we may pause your website until your account is up to date.

We send an invoice with payment details for every payment.

5. What we need from you

To finish your website on time we need your content (logo, wording, photos and any login details we need) and your feedback when we ask for it.

You confirm that you own, or have permission to use, everything you give us to put on your website. You are responsible for the accuracy of the information on your website.

6. Timelines

Most websites take 1 to 2 weeks, counted from when we have received your first payment and your content. Bigger or custom projects can take longer, and we agree the timeline with you before we start.

Timelines are estimates, given in good faith. If content or feedback arrives late, the launch date moves by the same amount of time.

7. Changes and revisions

Launch includes 1 round of changes. Launch Pro includes 2 rounds of changes. For other packages, the number of rounds is confirmed before work starts.

A round of changes is one combined list of adjustments to the agreed design. Further rounds, new pages or new features are extra work and are quoted before we do them.

8. Domain, hosting and other providers

Once-off packages cover the design and build of your website. Your domain name, hosting and any platform subscription (Framer, WordPress hosting or GoDaddy) are paid by you, directly to that provider.

Those providers have their own terms and prices, which we do not control. We are not responsible for their downtime, price changes or changes to their service.

9. Who owns the website

Your content is always yours. Your logo, wording, photos and brand remain your property.

Once-off packages. When you have paid in full, the finished website and its design belong to you. Until then, they remain the property of Rato Studio.

Monthly packages. The website remains the property of Rato Studio and is provided to you for as long as your subscription is active. When the subscription ends, the website is taken offline.

A domain name registered in your name stays yours in every case. Fonts, images, templates or plugins that come from other companies stay under those companies' licences.

10. Cancelling and refunds

Once-off packages. You may cancel at any time by email. The deposit is not refundable once work has started. If you cancel partway through, you pay for the work done up to the date you cancel.

Monthly packages. There is no minimum term. You may cancel at any time by giving us 30 days' notice by email. Your subscription, and your website, run until the end of the notice period. Monthly payments already made are not refunded.

We may end our work with you, on written notice, if payments are not made or if a website is used for anything unlawful.

11. Support after launch

Launch includes 14 days of support after your website goes live. Launch Pro includes 30 days. During this time we fix, at no charge, anything that does not work as agreed.

After that, or for new requests, work is quoted before we do it. For monthly packages, the support included is confirmed before work starts.

Nothing in these terms takes away rights you have under South African law, including the Consumer Protection Act.
